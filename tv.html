Buying Guidance

Buy with confidence.

General guidance to get you started. We’ll refine it around your home when you visit.

  • 01

    Match size to distance

    As a rough guide for 4K, sit about 1–1.5 times the screen diagonal away from the TV.

  • 02

    Pick the panel for your room

    Brighter rooms favour high-brightness LED/QLED; darker rooms show off OLED contrast.

  • 03

    Think about refresh rate

    A 100/120 Hz panel helps with fast sports and gaming; 60 Hz is fine for most everyday viewing.

  • 04

    Check the ports

    Count the HDMI ports you need for consoles, soundbars and set-top boxes.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What TV size should I buy for my room?

It depends on how far you sit. A common rule of thumb for 4K is roughly 1–1.5 times the screen diagonal. Our team can help you choose after learning about your room.

What is the difference between LED, QLED and OLED?

LED TVs use a backlight; QLED adds a quantum-dot layer for brighter, more vivid colour; OLED pixels light themselves, giving deep blacks and strong contrast.

Do I need a 4K TV?

4K is now the common standard for streaming and larger screens. On smaller screens in bedrooms, Full HD can still be a sensible, budget-friendly choice.
