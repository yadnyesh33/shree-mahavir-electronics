Buying Guidance

Buy with confidence.

General guidance to get you started. We’ll refine it around your home when you visit.

  • 01

    Pick capacity by household

    As a general guide: ~6–7 kg suits 2–4 people; 8 kg and above suits larger families or bulky bedding.

  • 02

    Front-load vs top-load

    Front-load tends to be gentler and more water-efficient; top-load is typically quicker and easier to load mid-cycle.

  • 03

    Look for an inverter motor

    It usually means quieter running and better durability.

  • 04

    Check the programs you will use

    Quick wash, delicates and heavy-duty cycles cover most real-life needs.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Which is better, front-load or top-load?

Neither is universally better. Front-load machines are usually gentler and more water-efficient; top-load machines are generally faster and easier to load.

What capacity should I choose?

Roughly 6–7 kg suits many families of 2–4. Choose higher if you wash bulky items like quilts regularly.

Do I need special plumbing?

Most machines need a water inlet and drain point. Share your setup with us and we can tell you what to check before delivery.

Can I enquire about specific models?

Absolutely — use Enquire Now or WhatsApp us for current availability.
