Buying Guidance

Buy with confidence.

General guidance to get you started. We’ll refine it around your home when you visit.

  • 01

    Match tonnage to room size

    Indicatively: ~1 ton for up to ~120 sq ft, ~1.5 ton for ~120–180 sq ft, ~2 ton for ~180–250 sq ft. Sun exposure and top-floor rooms may need more.

  • 02

    Choose inverter for regular use

    If the AC runs many hours a day, an inverter model typically pays back through lower power use.

  • 03

    Check the condenser & warranty

    Ask about condenser material and compressor warranty.

  • 04

    Plan the installation

    Confirm outdoor-unit placement, pipe length and a dedicated power point before purchase.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What tonnage AC do I need?

A rough guide is 1 ton for small rooms, 1.5 ton for medium rooms and 2 ton for larger rooms, adjusted for sunlight, ceiling height and number of occupants.

Is an inverter AC worth it?

For daily, long-hour use, inverter ACs generally consume less electricity and run more quietly than fixed-speed models.

Split or window AC?

Split ACs are quieter and more flexible in placement. Window ACs are simpler and often lower in upfront cost.

How do I get an AC installed?

Ask us about installation during your enquiry so we can explain what is involved and what to prepare.
