Buying Guidance

Buy with confidence.

General guidance to get you started. We’ll refine it around your home when you visit.

  • 01

    Estimate capacity

    As a general guide: small households often manage with up to ~250 L; families of 3–4 usually look at ~250–350 L; larger families at 350 L and above.

  • 02

    Choose a door style

    Single-door for compact needs, double-door for balanced convenience, side-by-side if you have room and need capacity.

  • 03

    Compare cooling type

    Frost-free models avoid manual defrosting; direct-cool models are simpler and typically more affordable.

  • 04

    Check star rating & warranty

    Higher BEE star ratings use less power. Ask about compressor warranty length.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What size refrigerator do I need?

It depends on household size and shopping habits. Many families of 3–4 find ~250–350 L comfortable. We can tailor advice to your routine.

Inverter or non-inverter?

Inverter compressors vary their speed, which usually means quieter operation and better efficiency over time.

What is the difference between frost-free and direct-cool?

Frost-free fridges automatically prevent ice build-up. Direct-cool fridges need occasional manual defrosting but are simpler and often cheaper.
